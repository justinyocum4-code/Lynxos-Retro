    Controls: hold the remote sideways, top to the left. The D-pad moves: right arrow is up, down arrow is forward, left arrow is down, up arrow is back. Channel up is A, channel down is B. Tap OK for Start and pause. Tap Back to roll. Hold Back and press OK for Y, channel up for L, channel down for R. Double tap channel down to hold run on or off. PlayStation: channel up is Cross, channel down is Circle, tap Back is Triangle, hold Back and press OK for Square, channel up for L1, channel down for R1. Hold Back alone for half a second to leave a game. Or pair a Bluetooth gamepad. Quick save is 1, quick load is 2.

    Account and cloud sync

    Sign in with email and password and your game library syncs between your tablet and your Google TV. A game added on the tablet shows up on the TV. When you sign up, we send a confirmation email — tap the link in it to finish signing up.

    Reset your password

    Enter your email in the Email box above, then tap "Send reset email". You will get an email with a link. Tap the link and come back here to choose a new password.
