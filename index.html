    Account and cloud sync

    Sign in with email and password and your game library syncs between your tablet and your Google TV. A game added on the tablet shows up on the TV. When you sign up, we send a confirmation email — tap the link in it to finish signing up.

    Reset your password

    Enter your email in the Email box above, then tap "Send reset email". You will get an email with a link. Tap the link and come back here to choose a new password.
